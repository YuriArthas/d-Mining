Use case: stylized-concept
Goal: A completely new, densely composed, finished 3D environment for a modern Roblox-style mining game. This replaces an abandoned sparse green field. Design a compact crafted mining courtyard, with beautiful coherent individual assets that can be reconstructed with image-to-3D.
Camera: Playable third-person establishing view, camera 3 metres above level ground, wide lens, looking slightly down across the courtyard. No isometric diorama, no aerial view, no characters.
Gameplay: A large square mining bed is centered in the courtyard, exactly 16 metres on each side. Its front approach is clear; the other three sides have thick smooth weathered stone retaining edges. There is no raised spawn platform. Keep 3-4 metre walkable routes to a right-hand ore selling counter and the left workshop.
Architecture: On the left, a substantial charming mine workshop with curved deep-teal tiled roofs, stone lower walls, carved timber supports, a large attached waterwheel, and an ore cart garage. On the right, a complete trading house with warm terracotta tiled roofs, cream stone walls, heavy curved timber brackets, a deep open porch with a visible counter, sacks and baskets of mineral specimens. Buildings are close to the mining pit and visually fill the sides; no detached tiny houses floating on an empty field.
Environment: Behind the pit, sculpted layered warm-gray limestone cliffs enclose the courtyard, with a narrow waterfall and timber footbridge. Dense sculpted broadleaf trees with leafy irregular crowns, banks of ferns and ground-cover plants, embedded boulders, carts, stacked timber and working props occupy the edges in deliberate groups. Foreground corners have low stone planters and dense foliage framing the open player route. Natural packed earth and inset stone pavers connect continuously to the building foundations; all foundations sink into the earth with no gaps, no thin floating paths, no modular seams.
Visual language: Clearly stylized premium modern Roblox adventure game, refined sculpted forms, rounded crafted edges, detailed but readable silhouettes, hand-painted material variation and real thickness. Not a photoreal photograph, not primitive cubes, not balloon-sphere trees. Warm cream stone, rust-red and teal roofs, golden wood, rich green foliage. Bright blue sky and soft full white clouds; warm sunlight, cool rich shadow fill, gentle contact occlusion. No gray wash, no chrome grass, no shiny plastic terrain, no thick fog.
Composition: Compact, lived-in, full foreground/midground/background depth. Main routes remain open while nearly every courtyard border has meaningful architecture or planted banks. No vast bare lawns.
Constraints: No UI, text, logos, watermarks, characters, tools held by players, backpacks or pets. One image of the complete coherent environment, not a collage or sheet.
