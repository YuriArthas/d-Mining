Use case: stylized-concept
Goal: Production concept for a fully rebuilt second-level underground mine rest station in a modern Roblox mining simulator. Match Roblox/Minecraft-inspired chunky construction, not Warcraft, not realistic fantasy.
Scene: A compact square cavern 40m wide by 40m deep by 20m high, dark slate block walls. Center is a precisely square 16x16m excavatable stone pit, flush with the surrounding floor, with short timber fences and open access at the front. Thick square honey-wood mine columns, straight beams and stepped braces built from 2m block units. Warm wood, blue slate, sparse copper ore accents.
Composition: Entrance/teleport landing in foreground center at (0,0,13); a clear 4m circulation route around the central pit. Left foreground is a new wooden ore-buying counter with amber awning, crates and weighing scale. Right side has short rail track, a loaded mine cart, squared tool racks. At rear center, a large symmetric block-built mine hoist with big dark wheel, taut ropes, crossbeam and hanging ore cage above rear platform, not over the central mine opening. Four strongly legible support frames at side walls. Several small wall lamps.
Camera: Third-person game camera at 3m height, 24mm lens, looking from south entrance toward the rear north wall. No player character.
Materials: Clearly pixel-textured chunky timber and coarse stone, restrained texture sizes, opaque matte surfaces, crisp broad planes with tiny edge chamfers. No fine realistic grain, no shiny stone, no thin branches, no random rounded boulders.
Lighting: Comfortable readable cave with cool slate ambient fill and localized amber pools near lamp fixtures. Lamps glow but scene is not yellow everywhere. Darker ceiling and cool recessed wall panels, bright readable walking floor. No dramatic black shadows or fog hiding geometry.
Constraints: The entire central 16x16 square and vertical volume above it remain open for digging and falling. Everything is within the 40x40 room. No vegetation, no water, no UI, no illegible writing, no logos, no watermark. It must look like a cohesive high-quality Roblox simulator level built from modular assets, not a realistic dungeon.
Deliverable: One landscape game-environment concept image, concrete buildable layout and clear blocky silhouettes.
